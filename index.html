01About

Analytical thinking, applied to real business problems.

I work at the point where a business question meets a dataset. Given a problem — a pricing decision, a churn risk, a question about which reviews to trust — I pull the relevant data, build the model or dashboard that answers it, and translate the result into a recommendation someone can act on the same week.

That's meant writing the SQL and Python to get from raw data to a clean dataset, building the machine learning models that sit underneath a forecast or classification, and putting the result in front of a stakeholder through a Power BI dashboard that answers the question rather than just displaying numbers. I'm equally comfortable in the analysis and in the decision it leads to — that combination of business analytics, data visualization, and decision making is what I bring to a team.
